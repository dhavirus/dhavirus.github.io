How this is calculated

BMR (Mifflin–St Jeor): men = 10×weight + 6.25×height − 5×age + 5; women = 10×weight + 6.25×height − 5×age − 161.

TDEE = BMR × activity multiplier. Target kcal shifts TDEE by ~7,700 kcal per kg/week of the chosen rate, never dropping below BMR.

Protein = your chosen g/kg. Fat = 25% of target kcal. Carbs = remainder.
